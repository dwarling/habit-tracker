# Problem
I want help building habits. With the stress of school and home life, finding the time
or energy to take care of myself can feel like a chore. Without a tool to keep yourself
in check, it's easy to fall out of habits. I hear that it takes roughly two months to
make a habit, and in that time, it's too easy to go back to what you're used to.
Something that keeps a feeling of achievement throughout the day could help me build
those habits.

# Initial requirements
- R1: The user can add a habit.
- R2: The user can remove a habit.
- R3: Each habit can be checked off once per day.
- R4: All checkmarks reset at midnight on the computer's clock.
- R5: The user is congratulated each time they check off a habit.
- R6: Each habit has its own 60-day goal, and every day it is checked off counts
  toward that goal.
- R7: Missing a day does not reset progress toward the goal, but that day does not count.
- R8: The user is congratulated when a habit reaches its 60-day goal.
- R9: Habits, checkmarks, and progress are saved, so they are still there after the
  browser is closed or the app is restarted.
